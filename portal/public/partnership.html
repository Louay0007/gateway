01 — Programme

Three ways to work with us

Every track shares the same product and the same gateway. They differ in who holds the customer relationship and how much support you take on.

/ 01

Referral

You introduce a team that needs reliable Claude access. We handle onboarding, keys, billing and support end to end.

  • 20% of subscription revenue, recurring for 12 months
  • Attribution by tracked link or account note
  • No technical commitment on your side

/ 02

Solution

You build and operate integrations — an internal tool, a product feature, a client deployment. Zaino is the inference layer underneath.

  • 25% recurring share, or wholesale per-seat pricing
  • Sandbox key with production-equivalent limits
  • Named technical contact and pre-release model access

/ 03

Infrastructure

You resell capacity at volume under your own brand, with committed monthly spend and a contractual service level.

  • Wholesale rates, agreed per committed volume
  • White-label base URL and invoicing
  • Custom daily budgets beyond 100M per tier

03 — Support

What you get to work with

The same gateway our own customers use — no partner edition, no reduced model list, no separate rate card.

Both wire formats, one key

OpenAI's /v1/chat/completions and Anthropic's /v1/messages against a single base URL, so existing SDKs and Claude Code work unchanged.

Ceilings that hold

Every plan carries a hard cap enforced at the gateway. Over the ceiling you get a clean error — never a surprise invoice at the end of the month.

Auditable metering

Every request is a ledger row: tokens by class, cost, model and status. Partners get the same export the customer does, so reconciliation is not a negotiation.

Local payment rails

D17 transfer through La Poste is a first-class payment method, verified by a human in under 24 hours. No card required, no FX surprise for the customer.

Usage you can show a client

Spend, tokens and per-model breakdown, exportable to CSV. Useful when a client asks what they are actually paying for.

A named contact

A shared channel with the people who operate the gateway — not a ticket queue. Escalations reach an engineer during Tunis business hours.
