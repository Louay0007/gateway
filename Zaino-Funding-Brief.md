# Zaino AI — How to fund the first 2,000 DT

Compiled 13 November 2026. Every contact, deadline and figure here was checked
against a live source that day; sources are listed in the workbook's Sources
sheet and at the end of this document.

---

## The short version

You asked for investors. After digging through the Tunisian ecosystem, I think
that is the wrong instrument for what you actually need.

You need roughly **1,900–3,000 DT** (≈$600–960) to incorporate, enrol in the
Apple Developer Program and get signing certificates. That is too small for a
venture round and too specific for an angel — no investor wants to write a
cheque that size, and the ones who would are really making you a grant.

But there is a mechanism in Tunisian law built almost exactly for your
situation, and it pays **12,000 DT over twelve months**.

It is called the **Bourse Startup**. Under the Startup Act, a founder who is a
shareholder in a labelled startup and works in it full-time receives a monthly
allowance from ANETI — 1,000 DT net per month for a non-salaried founder, up
to 5,000 DT for someone coming off a salary, for up to twelve months. Up to
three founders can claim it, each on their own application.

Twelve thousand dinars is four to six times what you were asking for, and it
is a grant: no equity, no repayment, no investor on your cap table.

The catch is the ordering. The Bourse requires the **Startup Label**, the Label
requires an **incorporated company**, and incorporation costs money you do not
have. That circularity is the real problem to solve — not fundraising.

So the plan is: raise about 2,000 DT from micro-grants that do not require a
company, use it to incorporate, get the Label, then claim the Bourse. Every
step is achievable within roughly three months.

One thing worth correcting in your plan: you listed "signing certificates" as a
separate cost. For Apple platforms it is not — the $99 membership includes the
certificates. You only need to buy one separately if you plan to distribute a
*signed Windows desktop build*, and even then it is optional at this stage. That
removes a line item you thought you had to fund.

## The sequence that works

**Week 1 — apply for everything that does not need a company.**

Emergent Ventures is the single best fit and you should do it first. It is a
rolling grant from the Mercatus Center, open to anyone aged 13+ anywhere in the
world, with a dedicated Africa and Caribbean track. Amounts range from about
$1,000 to $50,000, there is no equity, no company required, and decisions
typically arrive in one to three weeks. The application is a 1,500-word proposal
through a web form. Ask for a specific, modest number tied to your actual
budget — roughly $600–1,000 — rather than a round figure. Small, concrete and
clearly transformative is exactly the bar they describe in their own words.

Alongside it: the Awesome Foundation ($1,000, monthly, no strings), and the
self-serve cloud credit stack — AWS $1,000, Google $2,000, Microsoft up to
$5,000, Cloudflare $10,000. Credits are not cash, but you run a VPS and an
inference gateway, so they remove real monthly cost, which is the same thing as
money.

And **TotalEnergies Startupper of the Year closes 30 November 2026** — 17 days
from now. It is EUR 15,000, non-dilutive, and Tunisia is one of the 32
participating countries. There is an "under 3 years" track. Verify whether they
accept a not-yet-incorporated project, but apply regardless; it is the largest
open window right now.

**Week 1 — file the pre-label.**

Most people miss this. Article 4 of Law 2018-20 creates a **pre-label**
specifically for individuals who have not incorporated yet. It is valid six
months and exists to let you prove the innovation before you have a company. It
does *not* itself grant the tax exemption or the Bourse — that is a common
misunderstanding worth avoiding — but it starts your clock and puts you in front
of the labelling committee early, which is worth a lot when the Label is the
gate to everything else.

**Weeks 2–4 — incorporate.**

As soon as any grant lands. You have six months of pre-label; do not spend them.

**Weeks 4–6 — apply for the Label.**

100 DT fee, submitted only while a labelling session is open, so check the
portal weekly. Decision within 30 days; if they are silent for 60 days it counts
as approval. You already have the proof of concept they require, which puts you
ahead of most applicants.

**Within one month of the Label — file for the Bourse.**

This is a hard deadline and it is the one thing in this document you cannot
recover from. The application must be submitted within one month of receiving
the Label, and never more than one year after incorporation. Miss it and the
12,000 DT is gone permanently.

Check you meet the conditions first: Tunisian nationality, resident in Tunisia,
working full-time in the startup, not a student or doctoral candidate, not
already on another ANETI programme, and no Bourse in the previous three years.

**Then, and only then, the accelerators.**

Flat6Labs Tunis, 216 Capital and Carthage Business Angels all require a legal
entity. 216 Capital's accelerator is explicit: pre-seed, at least 50% of
headcount in Tunisia, and an established legal entity in Tunisia. You meet every
condition except the last one — which is why applying today would be an
automatic rejection, and why incorporation comes first.

## The uncomfortable thing you should know

The Startup Act benefits — the tax exemption, the Bourse, the FX account — all
attach to the **Label**, not to incorporation. A company without the Label gets
none of them. That is why the sequence above is ordered the way it is, and why
the 100 DT Label fee is the highest-return 100 DT in this entire plan.

The second uncomfortable thing: your business, as currently structured, resells
model access. The upstream terms permit powering your own product but forbid
resale without written approval, and your upstream is itself a reseller. That
does not block any application in this workbook — no grant programme asks — but
it will surface in diligence the moment you talk to 216 Capital or Flat6Labs.
Getting a written answer before then is worth more than any single cheque here.
If approval is not forthcoming, the private/team-internal positioning needs no
approval at all and is a perfectly fundable story.
