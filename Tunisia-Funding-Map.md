## 1. The bottom line

Your legal status eliminates roughly two thirds of the list, and it eliminates them
for the *same* reason: almost every Tunisian accelerator requires a registered
company. That is not a detail to discover later — it is the primary sort key.

So the map is:

| Tier | Meaning | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Open now, pre-incorporation accepted | **2** |
| **B** | Opens in a known window you can diarise | **3** |
| **C** | Good fit but **blocked on incorporation** | **6** |
| **D** | Not eligible — stop spending time here | **5** |

**The single most important finding:** the Startup Act **pré-label** submission window
is **1–8 December 2026**. Today is the 18th, so you have missed November and the next
window is 13 days away. That is the only instrument in Tunisia built *specifically*
for "no company yet", it costs 100 DT, and your repo already has the full dossier
prepared (`STARTUP-ACT-CONTEXT.md`).

## 2. Tier A — apply now

### A1. Startup Act Pré-label — the one that matters
- **Window:** 1st–8th of every month. Next: **1–8 December 2026**
- **Portal:** `apply.startupact.tn` / `portail.startup.gov.tn`
- **Cost:** 100 DT, payable at submission
- **Requirements:** no company needed; concept, business model, pitch, and a **product
  demo or POC**. Your suite of 1,130 tests and a live gateway is far past that bar.
- **Vote:** 5 favourable opinions out of 9 college members
- **Quota:** 20 pré-label places per session — founders report they go **in minutes**,
  faster than the Label places. Be at the keyboard at opening.
- **Rule:** one application per candidate per 6 months. Do not burn it on a rushed file.
- **Then:** 6 months to incorporate and convert to the Label.

⚠️ **One genuinely open question** flagged in `STARTUP-ACT-CONTEXT.md` §2 that no
public page answers: whether a **student** may hold a pré-label. You are a final-year
student at ISSAT Mateur. Ask `hello@startup.gov.tn` before 1 December, not after. The
Bourse Startup (12,000 DT) explicitly excludes students — the pré-label rule is not
published anywhere I could find.

### A2. SMU Incubator — 6th cohort
- **Deadline: 6 December 2026** — 18 days out
- **Explicitly accepts idea stage**, so no-company is not a barrier
- 6 months; engineering/business/health expertise, Maker Space, co-working,
  investor connections
- **Location:** SMU Incubator Center, Lac 2, Tunis
- **Apply:** `forms.cloud.microsoft/pages/responsepage.aspx?id=0rdWlefyskOgtaoX-SZ67WWs0R4HZStBtWm5WDdlfNNUQ09BOVRSU0pGVVBSNUZDU1pTV0tJVjJJQi4u`
- **Source:** `smu.tn/smu/news/applications-now-open-6th-cohort-smu-incubator` (posted 11 Nov 2026)

## 4. Tier C — right fit, blocked on incorporation

These are your **post-Label pipeline**, in the order I would work them.

### C1. AIR grant — 30,000 DT, non-dilutive
- **This is the biggest single prize available to you in Tunisia.**
- Requires: an **existing Tunisian legal entity less than 1 year old** at application,
  pre-existing team, POC-stage technology, strong growth potential.
- **Being enrolled in an SSO/POC programme is scored favourably** — so doing SMU or
  BIATLABS *first* directly improves this application.
- Disbursed 67% at contract signature, 33% on validated results + audited expenses.
- Results ~3 months after applying. Requires RNE extract <3 months old, 3-min pitch

- **Deadline pressure:** the "less than 1 year old" clock starts at incorporation. Apply

## 8. Honest assessment

**The good news** is unusual for a founder in your position: the pré-label exists
precisely for you, it needs no company, and your evidence bar — a running multi-tenant
gateway, 1,130 tests, 43 migrations, a live payment rail — is far above the "POC
minimum" the college requires. Most applicants at pré-label stage have a deck. You have
a product. That is a real and defensible advantage.

**The bad news** is that Tunisia's accelerator layer is almost entirely built for
companies that already exist. Of 16 programmes checked, exactly **one** (SMU) is open
today and accepts you without a legal entity. Six good ones are gated on incorporation,
and the largest single cheque available (AIR, 30,000 DT) requires a company under one
year old — meaning incorporation is not just a legal formality, it is the key that
unlocks the entire funding ladder at once.

Which reframes the goal. **You do not need an accelerator. You need 1,885 DT and a
RNE registration.** Every serious programme on this list becomes available the day you
have them. The search you asked for confirms the strategy already in your repo, from
the other direction.

**Two things to correct before you send anything:**
- `Tunisia-Contacts-Verified.md` says no window is open. SMU's closes **6 December**. Add it.
- The draft email in that file still quotes **641 tests**. The real figure is **1,130**.
  Numbers a reviewer can check are the cheapest credibility you own — keep them current.
