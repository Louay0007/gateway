## What I'd actually do

Stop applying. Start emailing — three a day, personalised, asking a question
rather than pitching.

Then fix the structural problem: you keep discovering calls **after** they
close. Two things solve that permanently.

**Join TunisianStartups** (join@tunisian-startups.com). They broadcast calls for
applications before they close. That is the cheapest fix available.

**Do the SNEE.** You are enrolled at ISSAT Mateur, so you have a route into the
Pôle Étudiant Entrepreneur at Carthage that outsiders do not. Eligibility is
continuous rather than annual, it costs nothing, and it puts you on the
university's mailing list for every competition and grant that comes through.

## Two corrections to what I told you earlier

**Founder Institute Tunis closed 10 November 2026** — four days before you
asked. Be aware of what it is anyway: $599 entrance fee plus a 2.5% equity
pledge to their Equity Collective, and it does not write cheques. For someone
who needs 1,885 DT to incorporate, paying $599 to join would be backwards.

**Orange Corners OCIF** pays up to 100,000 TND (grant + interest-free loan), but
it requires you to be *already generating revenue and officially registered in
Tunisia*. That is the opposite of your situation, and the third cohort closed on
30 August 2026. It is a 2027-2028 target, not a 2026 one.
